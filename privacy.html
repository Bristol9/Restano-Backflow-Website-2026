Information you provide

When you call or email Restano Backflow Testing, you may provide contact details, a service address, property information, an annual testing notice, scheduling preferences and other information needed to respond to your request.

Information collected automatically

The website itself does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. The website host may automatically record limited technical information such as an IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs for operation, reliability and fraud prevention.

How information is used

  • Responding to inquiries and scheduling requests
  • Providing estimates, testing services and compliance documentation
  • Communicating about appointments, records and payments
  • Maintaining website security and business records
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations

Payments

Major credit cards are accepted. Card details are handled through the applicable payment process or payment provider and are not collected through this public website. The payment provider’s own privacy and security terms may apply.

Sharing information

Information may be shared with water authorities, compliance databases, service providers or professional advisers when reasonably necessary to perform requested services, submit testing results, process payments, maintain records or comply with law. Personal information is not sold for advertising.

Retention and security

Business records are retained only as reasonably necessary for service, compliance, accounting and legal purposes. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
