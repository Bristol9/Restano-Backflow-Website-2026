Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

This page explains the limited browser storage used by this website and how to manage your preference.

Last updated August 6, 2026

Current website use

This website currently does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. It uses essential browser storage to remember whether you have responded to the cookie notice and which preference you selected.

Essential storage

The cookie preference is stored locally in your browser under a Restano website preference key. It is used only to avoid repeatedly showing the notice and does not identify you to the business.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may create technical logs needed to deliver and secure the website. These may include IP address, device or browser information, timestamps and requested pages. Such logs are controlled by the hosting provider and may not be browser cookies.

Optional technologies

No optional analytics or marketing technology is currently activated. If that changes, the notice and this policy should be updated before those technologies are enabled.

Managing your choice

You can reopen the preference panel using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. You may also clear website data through your browser settings.
